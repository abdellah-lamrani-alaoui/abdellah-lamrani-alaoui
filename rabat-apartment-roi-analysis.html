Rabat Apartment Investment Analysis

Where to buy for the best return on investment while staying in a safe, liquid neighborhood — built from market reports plus real, size-matched listing comps from Mubawab and Avito.

Research date: June 30, 2026 · For informational purposes — verify all figures against live listings before committing capital
Top pick

Buy a small studio or T1 (25–40 m²) in Agdal

Best evidence-backed balance of yield and safety in Rabat. Real comps show ~5–8% gross yield on small units in an established, secure, high-demand neighborhood — 15–20% cheaper per m² than Hay Riad with comparable quality of life. Important: this yield premium is concentrated in small units — a family-size 2–3BR Agdal apartment yields about the same as Hay Riad (~4.2–4.7%), so unit size is part of the strategy, not an afterthought.

Practical buying strategy

  1. Unit type: a small, efficient studio/T1 (25–40 m²) outperforms a large family apartment on DH/m² yield.
  2. Sub-location: Bas Agdal = cheaper, rents well to students/young workers (max yield); Haut Agdal = calmer, pricier, suits longer-term tenants (max stability).
  3. Proximity to tramway: stay within 5–10 min walk of a stop — the single biggest driver of yield and resale liquidity.
  4. Furnished vs. unfurnished: furnished rentals to professionals/expats command a real premium; weigh against furnishing cost (~50,000–100,000 DH).
  5. Holding period: plan for 5–7 years to capture both rental income and appreciation; transaction costs (~6–7% of price) make a sub-2-year flip unattractive.
  6. Due diligence: confirm clean titre foncier, check syndic charges, verify no unresolved inheritance (indivision) — common in older Agdal buildings.

Portfolio framing

  • Conservative core: Hay Riad apartment rented to an expat/executive — lower yield (~4.5%), best capital protection and liquidity.
  • Core ROI pick: Agdal studio/T1 — 5–8% yield, low risk. This analysis's top recommendation.
  • Satellite growth bet: Aviation — undervalued today, tramway-driven appreciation likely over 5–7 years.
  • Avoid as primary holding: Yacoub El Mansour, Akkari, Salé — nominal yield isn't statistically reliable in the real comp data and carries materially more execution risk.

Methodology & limitations

No photo/visual verification was possible. A headless browser (Playwright/Chromium) was blocked by this environment's network policy from reaching Avito/Mubawab/Yakeey directly, and the WebFetch tool was blocked by those sites' own anti-bot protection. All matching here is therefore text-based (size, room count, floor, condition keywords like "rénové"/"bon état") rather than photo-verified. One Aviation comp pair implied an improbable 13.7% yield and was discarded as a likely condition mismatch — exactly the kind of error photo verification would normally catch. Treat all figures as directional, and inspect units in person (or via a trusted local agent) before committing capital.
