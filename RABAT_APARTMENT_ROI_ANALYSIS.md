# Rabat Apartment Investment Analysis — Best ROI in Low-Risk Neighborhoods

*Research date: June 30, 2026. Prices are indicative market ranges (DH/m²) compiled from current listings/market reports — always verify against live listings and get a notary/avocat to check title (titre foncier) before committing.*

## TL;DR Recommendation

**Buy a small studio or T2 (1-2 bedroom) in Agdal**, ideally near a tramway stop, targeting students/young professionals or furnished short-term rental. It is the best balance of safety + yield in Rabat: established, secure, high rental demand, prices 15-20% below Hay Riad/Souissi, and gross yields of **5-8%** vs. 4-6% in the most prestigious districts.

If you want more upside and can tolerate slightly more risk, **Aviation** is the #2 pick: still undervalued, benefiting from tramway extension, good resale/appreciation potential, decent safety.

Avoid chasing the highest headline yields (Yacoub El Mansour, Akkari, Salé Tabriquet/Bettana ~6.3-7.5%) — those come with weaker tenant profiles, lower liquidity, and more volatile resale value, which is exactly the risk you said you want to avoid.

## 2. Why Agdal wins on your criteria ("highest ROI, still safe")

- **Yield**: small, well-located studios (25-30 m²) rent for 3,000-4,500 DH/month → 5-7% gross. Furnished T2s rent 6,000-7,500 DH/month → similarly strong yields, sometimes up to 8% on the smallest, most efficiently-bought units.
- **Demand depth**: students (nearby universities), young professionals, and executives all compete for Agdal units — this keeps vacancy low and supports rent growth, unlike riskier areas that depend on a single tenant segment.
- **Price discount vs. peers**: 15-20% cheaper per m² than Hay Riad or Souissi for comparable safety and services, which is what pushes the yield % higher without taking on real neighborhood risk.
- **Appreciation**: consistently cited as one of the fastest-appreciating central districts (alongside Hay Riad and Hassan), so you're not trading yield for stagnant resale value.

## 3. Practical buying strategy

1. **Unit type**: prioritize a small, efficient studio or T1/T2 (25-45 m²) over a large apartment — smaller units in Agdal post the best DH/m² yield because rent doesn't scale linearly down with size.
2. **Location within Agdal**: Bas Agdal (lower Agdal) is more commercial/affordable and rents well to students/young workers; Haut Agdal is calmer/pricier and suits longer-term family or executive tenants — pick based on whether you want max yield (Bas Agdal) or max stability (Haut Agdal).
3. **Proximity to tramway**: stay within a 5-10 min walk of a tram stop — this is repeatedly cited as the single biggest driver of both yield and liquidity at resale.
4. **Furnished vs. unfurnished**: furnished rentals to young professionals/expats command meaningfully higher rent in Agdal and Hassan; factor in furnishing cost (~50,000-100,000 DH) against the yield uplift.
5. **Financing/holding period**: a 5-7 year horizon is typically cited as needed to capture both rental income and capital appreciation — don't plan to flip in under 2 years given transaction costs (notary fees, registration ~6-7% of price).
6. **Due diligence**: confirm titre foncier (freehold title) is clean, check syndic/co-ownership charges, and verify the building isn't in an unresolved inheritance (indivision) situation — a common source of deal risk in older Agdal buildings.

## 4. If you want to diversify risk/return

- **Conservative core (lowest risk)**: Hay Riad apartment, rented to an expat/executive — lower yield (~5%) but best capital protection and easiest resale.
- **Core ROI pick (this analysis's top choice)**: Agdal studio/T2 — 5-8% yield, low risk.
- **Satellite / growth bet (higher upside, still acceptable risk)**: Aviation — undervalued today, tramway-driven appreciation likely over 5-7 years.
- **Avoid as a primary holding** unless you specifically want higher-risk/higher-yield exposure and can actively manage tenants: Yacoub El Mansour, Akkari, Salé.
