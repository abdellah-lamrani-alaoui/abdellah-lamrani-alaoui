Methodology. Listings were located via targeted, site-restricted, price-banded search queries rather than direct page scraping — a headless browser was blocked by this environment's network policy from reaching these sites directly, and the WebFetch tool was blocked by the sites' own anti-bot protection (both return 403). Prices, sizes, and amenities shown come from search-result summaries of each listing page, not a re-fetch of the page itself, so treat every figure as "last seen," not live — a few entries above could not be matched to a price at all and are marked accordingly. No photos were reviewed for any listing — confirm condition/standing in person or via a local agent before making an offer, and expect roughly 5-15% negotiation room off the asking price, which is standard in this market. The "DH/m²" column is a simple division of listed price by listed surface and does not adjust for floor, condition, or finish quality — treat large swings between similar-sized units in the same neighborhood (e.g. Guéliz 75 m² at 32,000 DH/m² vs. 234 m² at 10,300 DH/m²) as a flag to inspect, not a typo.
