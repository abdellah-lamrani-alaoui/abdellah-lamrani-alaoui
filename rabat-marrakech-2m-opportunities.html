Methodology. Listings were located via targeted search queries (site-restricted, price-banded) rather than direct page scraping — a headless browser was blocked by this environment's network policy from reaching these sites directly, and the WebFetch tool was blocked by the sites' own anti-bot protection (both return 403). Prices, sizes, and amenities shown come from search-result summaries of each listing page, not a re-fetch of the page itself, so treat every figure as "last seen," not live. No photos were reviewed — confirm condition/standing in person or via a local agent before making an offer, and expect 5-15% negotiation room off the asking price, which is standard in this market.
